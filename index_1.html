Reading these figures. Numbers are the raw counts reported in each publication, with a 95% Wilson confidence interval. Trial participants are selected (for example, most post-resuscitation trials enrolled comatose adults with a presumed cardiac cause who survived to ICU admission), so these are cohort averages, not a prognosis for an individual patient.

Withdrawal of life-sustaining therapy. Most of the trials allowed withdrawal after protocolised prognostication, which shapes both mortality and the share of survivors with severe disability. The Korean (KORHN-PRO) and Italian (ProNeCA) cohorts come from settings where withdrawal was rare or absent; they are observational and differ in case mix, so compare with care.

Outcome definitions differ between trials (mRS, CPC, GOS-E, Disability Rating Scale) and are given in the legend of each figure. Where a count had to be derived (from a percentage or by subtraction), the figure says so.
